# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific when discussing code. Critique ideas, explain evidence, and welcome contributors with different experience levels. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publishing another person's credentials or personal data are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Report conduct concerns involving private information through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). For technical vulnerabilities, use [the private security channel](SECURITY.md).
